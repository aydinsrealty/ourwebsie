Asset Synopsis

Conceived as an exclusive 50–60 member co-owned modern farmhouse estate, Aydins Heaven is not a commercial resort with artificial pools. It is an authentic living sanctuary designed especially for families and NRIs seeking hands-on soil connection, organic farming, gentle farm animals, and peaceful stays across 10–12 rustic-modern eco-cottages.
