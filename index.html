AYDINS' LAWS OF DEVELOPMENT

A deal that is 99% done is not done.

That 1% is everything.

Strategy changes with the street.

The town-centre investor and bypass commercial buyer are not the same person.

Your developer should know the zoning, the yield, and the exit.

Not just the architectural elevations.

If you have to lean out the window to see the mountain, it’s not a mountain view.

A good view should not require imagination.

Client matters are not public matters.

Discretion is part of the service.

If the lease is not understood, the asset is not understood.

The real value is written in the fine print.

The most expensive mistake is rushing.

The second most expensive is hesitating.

Trust makes the deal.

The contract protects it.

Most deals don’t fail on price.

They fail on expectations.

“Not in a rush” usually means “Not at that price.”

Timing often hides the real objection.

A comp without context is just gossip.

Numbers mean nothing without the story behind them.

Never make the brochure prettier than the building.

Tell the truth in words and media.

We develop high-potential landmarks on purpose.

You won’t find our best co-investment opportunities on public classifieds, and that’s by design.
